\chapter{Time Series Analysis \& Financial Engineering}
\label{ch:time-series}

Every model so far has assumed that observations are independent draws from a
fixed distribution.\projectref{projects/05\_time\_series/} Time series violate both halves of that assumption, and the
consequences are practical rather than academic: the random train/test split of
\cref{sec:validation} leaks future information into the past, and a model
validated that way will look excellent and lose money.

The chapter builds the classical statistical toolkit first, then the stochastic
calculus that finance is written in, and closes on the deep architectures that
now compete with both. Ito's lemma is the chain rule of
\cref{eq:chain-rule} extended to processes with non-vanishing quadratic
variation --- worth deriving carefully, because the Black--Scholes PDE follows
from it in a few lines.
